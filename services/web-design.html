Services · Canada & USA

Web design and development agency for businesses in Canada and the USA

Zynexor designs and builds websites that bring in enquiries, not just compliments. We work with service businesses, professional firms, startups and creators across Canada and the USA, and we handle everything from strategy and copy structure to design, development, SEO set-up and launch.

What our web design service includes

  • Strategy and structure: goals, audience, sitemap and the pages your customers need.
  • UX and visual design: wireframes and custom designs for desktop and mobile.
  • Development: WordPress, Webflow or custom code, chosen to fit how you will update the site. Read WordPress vs Webflow vs custom code.
  • Conversion features: online booking, contact forms, CRM integrations and live data.
  • SEO foundations: fast loading, clean structure, page titles, structured data and sitemaps.
  • Accessibility: designed and tested against WCAG 2.1 Level AA, which supports AODA and ADA obligations.
  • Launch and care: redirects from your old site, analytics, training and maintenance plans.

Websites that perform

Speed and clarity matter. The Zynexor website itself scores 100 for performance on desktop in Google PageSpeed Insights, and we apply the same techniques to client sites: compressed media, lazy loading and lean code.

Our process

  • Discover (week 1): a call, a short brief and research into your market.
  • Design (weeks 2–4): structure, wireframes and visual design, reviewed with you weekly.
  • Build (weeks 4–8): development, content entry and testing on real devices.
  • Launch and grow: go live, measure results and keep improving.

Most small-business websites take six to ten weeks.

Services · Canada & USA

Branding agency for growing businesses in Canada and the USA

Zynexor builds brands that help small businesses, startups and professional firms in Canada and the USA look established, stand out from competitors and charge what they are worth. We combine brand strategy with visual identity design, so your brand is both clear and memorable.

What our branding service includes

  • Brand strategy: customer research, positioning, value proposition, messaging and tone of voice.
  • Naming: name ideas with domain and trademark database checks (CIPO in Canada, USPTO in the USA).
  • Visual identity: logo, colour palette, typography, iconography and imagery style.
  • Brand guidelines: a practical guide your team and suppliers can follow.
  • Brand assets: business cards, presentation and proposal templates, social media templates and signage.
  • Website and launch support: applying the new brand to your website and social profiles.

Who it is for

  • New businesses and startups that need to look credible from day one.
  • Established businesses whose brand no longer matches the quality of their work.
  • Professional firms, such as law, consulting and healthcare practices, that need to build trust quickly.

Our branding process

  • Discover (week 1): we interview you and your customers and review your competitors.
  • Define (weeks 1–2): we agree positioning and messaging in plain language.
  • Design (weeks 2–4): we present visual directions, refine one and test it on real applications.
  • Deliver (weeks 4–6): final files, brand guidelines and templates, ready to use.

A focused branding project usually takes three to six weeks.

Recent branding work

We created the brand and launch website for SocialBoost AI, built a consistent social media look for Funpark Indoor Amusement Center, and designed the brand experience for the Style.Bd marketplace app. See our case studies and read our small business branding guide.

FAQ

How much does branding cost?

It depends on the scope: a logo refresh costs far less than a full strategy, naming and identity system. We send a written proposal with a fixed price after a free discovery call.

Do you work with businesses outside your cities?

Yes. We work remotely with clients across Canada and the USA, with video calls and weekly check-ins.

Will I own the logo and brand files?

Yes. On final payment you receive full ownership of all final brand files.

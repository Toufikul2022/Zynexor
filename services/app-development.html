Services · Canada & USA

Mobile app design and development for startups and businesses

Zynexor designs and builds mobile apps that people open again tomorrow. We help founders and businesses in Canada and the USA turn an idea into a tested product, from first prototype to App Store and Google Play launch.

What our app service includes

  • Product discovery: goals, users, core features and an MVP scope that fits your budget.
  • UX design: user flows, wireframes and clickable prototypes tested with real users.
  • UI design: polished visual design for iOS and Android, with a reusable design system.
  • Development: cross-platform or native apps, back ends, admin panels and integrations such as payments and notifications.
  • Launch: App Store and Google Play submission, analytics and crash reporting.
  • Growth: updates, new features and maintenance after launch.

Start with an MVP

We recommend launching the smallest version that delivers real value, then improving it with user feedback. It is faster, cheaper and less risky than building everything at once. Read How much does it cost to build a mobile app?.

FAQ

Do you build for iOS and Android?

Yes. We often recommend a cross-platform approach so you can launch on both with one codebase.

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused MVP usually takes about three to six months from design to launch, depending on features.

Will I own the code?

Yes. You own the code, designs and app store accounts.

UI/UX design for SaaS products, dashboards and web apps

Zynexor designs SaaS platforms, dashboards, CRMs and internal tools that are easy to learn and fast to use. We work with startups and established companies in Canada and the USA to reduce churn, cut support requests and make complex workflows feel simple.

What our UI/UX service includes

  • UX audit: a review of your current product with prioritised fixes.
  • User research: interviews, task analysis and usability testing.
  • Information architecture and user flows: how users move through the product.
  • Wireframes and prototypes: tested before development starts.
  • Visual design and design systems: consistent components, ready for developers.
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 Level AA, increasingly required in enterprise procurement.
  • Developer handoff: specs, assets and support during the build.

What we design

  • B2B SaaS platforms and admin panels.
  • Analytics dashboards and reporting tools. Read our dashboard UX best practices.
  • CRMs and internal tools that replace spreadsheets.
  • AI products where trust and clarity matter most. Read Designing trust into AI products.

The TrustLayer AI governance platform, with its AI tool registry, policy generator and trust portal, and a CRM for an immigration consultancy covering leads, clients, meetings and reports. See our case studies.

FAQ

Can you work with our in-house developers?

Yes. We deliver design systems and specs your developers can build from, and we join stand-ups during the build if helpful.

How long does a UX project take?

An audit takes one to two weeks. Designing a new product or major feature typically takes several weeks, depending on the number of screens and user roles.

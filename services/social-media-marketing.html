Social media marketing and content design for small businesses

Zynexor plans and designs social media campaigns that keep local businesses and brands visible and bring customers through the door. We work with businesses in Canada and the USA on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

What our social media service includes

  • Strategy: platform choice, content pillars, posting schedule and goals.
  • Campaign concepts: recurring offers, seasonal campaigns and launches.
  • Content design: post, carousel, story and reel designs that match your brand.
  • Templates: on-brand templates your team can use for quick updates.
  • Monthly content calendars: planned around holidays in Canada and the USA and your own key dates.
  • Paid promotion support: ad creatives for boosting your best posts.

How we work

We start with a strategy session, then deliver a monthly content plan and designs for your approval. Many clients post day-to-day updates themselves and use our templates and campaign designs for the big moments. Read our guide to social media marketing for small businesses.

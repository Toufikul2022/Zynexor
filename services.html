Questions and Answers

    

      
How long does it take to get started?
Usually one week from signing. We hold a kick-off meeting and start straight away.

      
Can I hire you for just one service?
Yes. Many clients start with a logo, a website or a set of social posts and add more later.

      
Do you work on a fixed-price basis or time and material?
Both. Clear scopes get a fixed price; ongoing work runs on a monthly retainer or time and material.

      
Who owns the designs and code?
You do. Once the project is paid, all source files, designs and code are handed over to you.

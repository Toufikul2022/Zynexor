Questions and Answers

How long does it take to get started?

Usually one week from signing. We hold a kick-off meeting, agree the first milestones and start straight away.

What is your typical working process?

We start with analysis: your goals, users and requirements. Then we share an estimate in a written proposal. Once it is approved we kick off, design and build in weekly cycles and launch together.

Do you work on a fixed-price basis or time and material?

Both. When the requirements are clear and detailed we quote a fixed price. For ongoing work such as social media or product development we use a monthly retainer or time and material.

How do you usually start new collaborations?

With an introduction call and a short brief about your project. Use the Contact button or email us and we will set up the call.

Do you work with big companies only? We're a small business.

Not at all. We work with founders, local businesses and growing companies. Our Launch engagement is built for teams that are just getting started.

How long will it take to get an estimate from you?

Usually two to three business days after the intro call, depending on how detailed the brief is.

Do you have a minimum engagement?

No strict minimum. Smaller jobs such as a logo or a set of social posts are welcome, and many clients add more services later.

What's the average project duration?

A brand and landing page takes 3 to 6 weeks, a full website 6 to 10 weeks and an app or dashboard release 8 to 16 weeks.

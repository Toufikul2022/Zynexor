SocialBoost AI: brand and launch website for an AI social post generator

Client
SocialBoost AI
Industry
AI / marketing technology
Services
Branding, Landing page, Web app UX/UI, Art direction

SocialBoost AI is an AI tool that writes scroll-stopping social media posts in seconds for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and TikTok.

The challenge

The product needed to launch with a brand that felt modern and trustworthy, a landing page that converted visitors into sign-ups, and a generator interface simple enough to use in seconds.

What we delivered

  • Brand identity: a confident visual system with purple gradients, rounded type and bold headlines.
  • Conversion-focused landing page: a clear promise, a free start and proof points near every call to action.
  • Post generator UX/UI: platform selection, post types and tone options in one clean screen.
  • Mobile experience: the same flow designed for phones.

Why it works

A consistent brand across the website, app and social posts means people recognise the product before they read its name. Read Why your brand needs a system, not just a logo.

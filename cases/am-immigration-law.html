A&M Canadian Immigration Law: a law firm website that makes immigration pathways clear

Client
A&M Canadian Immigration Law Corporation
Industry
Legal / immigration
Location
Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada
Services
Web design, Content design, Infographics, SEO

A&M Canadian Immigration Law Corporation is an immigration law firm in Winnipeg, Manitoba, helping individuals, families and businesses with permanent residence, temporary residence, citizenship, refusals and appeals.

The challenge

Immigration clients arrive anxious and confused by complex rules. The firm needed a website that explains its services clearly, shows its credentials and keeps clients up to date with fast-changing programs such as the Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program.

What we built

  • A services directory organised by client goals: urgent cases, permanent residence, temporary residence and US immigration.
  • A lawyer profile: credentials, experience and memberships, presented to build trust.
  • Live Manitoba PNP draw tables: draw dates, numbers and scores for each stream.
  • A news and case-law library: regular updates that give visitors a reason to return and give search engines fresh content.
  • Illustrated guides: roadmaps and mind maps that explain pathways at a glance.
  • Google reviews integration: real client reviews on the website.

Why it works

Every page starts from a real client question and ends with a clear next step: booking a consultation. Read more in Website design for immigration law firms in Canada and How a clear website turns visitors into clients.

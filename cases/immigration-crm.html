Immigration consultancy CRM: leads, clients and meetings in one place

Client
Immigration consultancy CRM
Industry
Legal / professional services
Services
Web app UX/UI, Dashboard design, Development

An immigration consultancy needed one tool to replace scattered spreadsheets, inboxes and calendars.

The challenge

Leads were slipping through the cracks and the team had no single view of clients, deals, tasks and revenue.

What we built

  • Leads and deals: a pipeline that follows each enquiry from first contact to signed client.
  • Clients and projects: every file, task and document in one place.
  • Meetings calendar: a monthly view with upcoming appointments.
  • Reports: revenue by month, lead sources and conversion by stage.

Why it works

We designed around the team's real workflow, so the CRM matches how people already work. Read From spreadsheets to one CRM and SaaS dashboard design best practices.

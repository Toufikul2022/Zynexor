Style.Bd: a mobile marketplace where live shopping meets verified sellers

Client
Style.Bd
Industry
Retail / e-commerce
Location
Bangladesh
Services
Mobile app design, UX/UI design, E-commerce flows, Prototyping

Style.Bd is a fashion marketplace app for buyers in Bangladesh, where most shopping starts on a phone and many purchases begin in a live video.

The challenge

Buyers needed to discover products, trust new sellers and buy without leaving the app, while sellers needed a way to showcase products live.

What we designed

  • Home and trending feeds: discovery built around patterns people know from social apps.
  • Live shopping: buyers can watch, comment and buy in one place.
  • Verified sellers: badges and profiles that reduce the worry of buying from someone new.
  • Shop and category browsing: fast paths to the right products.
  • In-app messaging: buyers and sellers can talk without switching apps.

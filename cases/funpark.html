Funpark: a social media campaign that keeps a family fun park top of mind

Client
Funpark Indoor Amusement Center
Industry
Leisure / family entertainment
Services
Social media design, Campaign concepts, Art direction

Funpark Indoor Amusement Center is a family entertainment venue with trampolines, slides and play areas.

The challenge

Funpark needed to stay visible every week, fill quieter days and promote seasonal moments without its feed looking repetitive.

What we created

  • Discount Tuesday: a recurring weekly offer that followers learn to expect and share.
  • Brand moments: "Bounce Mode", "All in One Place" and "Everything" posts that show the fun of the venue.
  • Seasonal campaigns: Christmas and New Year posts that keep the feed warm between offers.
  • Consistent art direction: big, readable type and real photography of kids and families.

Why it works

One clear idea per post, real people and a reason to act now. Read What makes a social post stop the scroll and our social media marketing guide.

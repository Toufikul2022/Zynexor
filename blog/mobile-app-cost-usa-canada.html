How much does it cost to build a mobile app in the USA and Canada?

"How much will my app cost?" is one of the most common questions we hear from founders and business owners in the USA and Canada. The answer depends on features, platforms and how polished the first version needs to be. This guide explains the main cost drivers and the rough ranges you are likely to see.

Rough market ranges

Quotes vary widely between freelancers, agencies and regions. As a broad guide:

  • Simple app or MVP: often in the tens of thousands of US dollars. A few core screens, basic user accounts and a simple back end.
  • Mid-complexity app: commonly from several tens of thousands to well over US$100,000. Payments, chat, notifications, an admin panel and integrations.
  • Complex app: live video, marketplaces, real-time features, heavy security or compliance can push budgets much higher.

What drives the cost

  • Number and complexity of features. Each feature needs design, front-end work, back-end logic and testing.
  • Platforms. iOS only, Android only, or both. Cross-platform frameworks such as React Native or Flutter can reduce the cost of building for both.
  • Design quality. A polished, tested user experience takes more design time but improves retention.
  • Back end and integrations. Payments, maps, CRMs, video streaming and third-party APIs all add work.
  • Security and compliance. Health, finance and children's apps need extra care.

Ongoing costs after launch

  • App store accounts: Apple's Developer Program costs US$99 per year, and Google Play charges a one-time US$25 registration fee.
  • Hosting and services: servers, databases, push notifications, email and analytics.
  • Maintenance: updates for new iOS and Android versions, bug fixes and security patches. A common rule of thumb is to budget a meaningful share of the original build cost each year.

How to reduce cost without cutting quality

  • Start with an MVP. Launch the smallest version that delivers real value, then grow it based on user feedback.
  • Prototype first. A clickable design prototype lets you test with users before paying for development.
  • Choose cross-platform when it fits. One codebase for iOS and Android often saves time.
  • Reuse proven services. Use established payment, login and messaging services instead of building them from scratch.

Example: Style.Bd

For Style.Bd, a fashion marketplace app with live shopping, we designed the full experience from discovery to checkout: home feed, live shopping, trending items, verified sellers and in-app messaging. Designing every flow before development made the build faster and more predictable. See the Style.Bd case study.

FAQ

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused MVP often takes around three to six months from design to launch. Larger apps take longer and are usually released in phases.

Should I build for iOS or Android first?

In the USA and Canada, iOS often has a strong share of paying users, but it depends on your audience. Cross-platform development lets you launch on both together.

Do I own the app code?

You should. Make sure your contract transfers full ownership of the code, designs and app store accounts to your company.

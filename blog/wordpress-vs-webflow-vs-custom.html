WordPress vs Webflow vs custom code: which is right for your business website?

Choosing the platform for your website affects its cost, speed, security and how easily your team can update it. Most small and mid-sized businesses in Canada and the USA end up choosing between WordPress, Webflow and a custom-coded site. Here is how they compare.

WordPress

WordPress is the most widely used website platform in the world. It is open source, so you can host it anywhere.

  • Best for: content-heavy sites, blogs, businesses that want full ownership and a huge choice of plugins.
  • Strengths: flexible, large community, many developers available, strong SEO plugins.
  • Watch out for: plugin overload, security updates and slower speeds if it is not built carefully. Budget for regular maintenance.

Webflow

Webflow is a hosted visual website builder that produces clean code and is popular with design-led teams.

  • Best for: marketing websites and landing pages where design quality and easy editing matter most.
  • Strengths: polished designs and animations, hosting and security handled for you, an easy editor for marketing teams.
  • Watch out for: ongoing subscription fees, less flexibility for complex functionality, and content that stays on Webflow's platform. Check current plan pricing on Webflow's site.

Custom code

A custom-coded website, for example a static site or a React-based site, is built specifically for your needs.

  • Best for: high-performance marketing sites, web applications, dashboards, client portals and products with unique features.
  • Strengths: maximum speed and flexibility, no unused plugins, easy to scale into a full web app.
  • Watch out for: you need developers for most changes unless a content management system is connected.

How to decide

  • Your team edits content every week: WordPress or Webflow.
  • Design and animation are the priority: Webflow or custom code.
  • You need user accounts, dashboards or complex integrations: custom code.
  • You want the lowest ongoing platform fees: WordPress or a custom static site with low-cost hosting.
  • Speed scores matter a lot: custom code or a carefully built Webflow or WordPress site.

FAQ

Is Webflow better than WordPress for SEO?

Both can rank very well. SEO depends far more on content, speed, structure and links than on the platform itself.

Can I move from Webflow or WordPress to custom code later?

Yes. Plan URL redirects carefully so you keep your search rankings during the move.

How to get more leads from your website: 10 fixes for service businesses

Many service businesses in Canada and the USA get decent website traffic but too few enquiries. The problem is rarely the number of visitors. It is what happens after they arrive. These ten fixes are the changes we make most often when a client asks us to turn their website into a lead generator.

1. Say what you do in the first screen

Visitors decide in seconds whether they are in the right place. Your first screen should state who you help, what you do and where, in plain words, with one clear button. "Immigration lawyers helping families move to Canada" beats "Excellence in legal solutions".

2. Use one main call to action

Choose the action that matters most, such as "Book a free consultation", and repeat it in the header, after key sections and at the end of every page. Too many competing buttons reduce clicks on all of them.

3. Let people book a time online

Many people would rather pick a time than wait for a reply. An embedded booking calendar, like the one we use with Calendly, removes back-and-forth emails and captures leads outside office hours.

4. Shorten your forms

Ask only what you need to start the conversation: name, email, phone if necessary, and a short message. Every extra field loses some people. Collect details later.

5. Show proof everywhere

Add reviews, client logos, case studies, certifications and numbers near your calls to action. Real names, photos and results build far more trust than general claims. See how we present work in our case studies.

6. Answer objections before they are asked

Price, timeline, process and "is this right for me?" are the usual hesitations. A clear process section and an FAQ near the bottom of key pages answer them and keep visitors moving.

7. Make the site fast on phones

Slow pages lose visitors before they see anything. Compress images and video, load only what is needed, and test with Google PageSpeed Insights. Most local searches happen on mobile.

8. Build a page for each service

A visitor looking for "website redesign" should land on a page about website redesign, not a general services list. Dedicated service pages convert better and rank for more searches.

9. Add tap-to-call and click-to-email

On mobile, your phone number and email should be one tap away. Make sure they work correctly and are tracked as conversions.

10. Track every lead

Set up analytics events for form submissions, bookings, calls and email clicks. Then you can see which pages and channels bring real enquiries and improve them over time.

A quick self-check

Open your website on your phone and ask: within five seconds, would a stranger know what you do, who it is for and what to do next? If not, start with fixes 1 and 2.

FAQ

How quickly will these changes increase leads?

Clearer messaging, booking and shorter forms can improve enquiries soon after launch. SEO-driven changes, such as new service pages, take longer to bring extra traffic.

Should I add live chat?

It can help if someone can reply quickly during business hours. An unanswered chat box does more harm than good.

Do I need a full redesign?

Not always. Many of these fixes can be made on your existing site. A redesign makes sense when the structure, platform or brand no longer fits your business.
